ExpressiveTool

How it comes to life

  • Page or tool

    One question decides the layout: does the person work through data? Then it’s a tool, dense from the top. Anything else is a page.

  • Lead with the title

    The title is the first thing on the screen, with nothing small above it. Navigation and the one action wait in the bottom slot.

  • A hero sized from the screen

    An object, a statement, media or the main view in a card, right under the title, taking a share of the screen rather than a fixed size.

  • The gap and the room

    48 between groups, everywhere. Whatever height is left goes below the hero, so a short page reads as composed, never padded.

  • Interaction is the constant

    Hover, tooltips and timing are the same on every page. A tool’s expression is its precision, never louder colour or more motion.

Use it

  1. Step 1Copy the tokens

    Start every project with tokens.css, verbatim. Every value on this site comes from it.

  2. Step 2Build it plain, then decide

    Semantic HTML first, no styling. Then ask the one question: a page or a tool?

  3. Step 3Give the hero the first screen

    The title, the hero under it, the room below. The rest follows at the gap.

  4. Step 4Reach for a pattern, then components

    Patterns arrange a piece of a page; components fill it. Copy them from the library as they ship.

  5. Step 5Check it where it will be seen

    A phone at 390px with touch, or a desktop with a mouse — whichever the person is using.
